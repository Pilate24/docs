GlobeTrotter is designed so you can go from a blank slate to a fully scoped multi-city itinerary in minutes. This Quick Start guide walks you through every step: signing up, creating a trip, adding city stops, scheduling activities, reviewing your budget, and sharing your plan with the world. Follow the steps in order and you will have a live, shareable itinerary by the end.

<Note>
  GlobeTrotter comes pre-seeded with **20+ global destinations** and **80+ curated activities** — covering everything from Eiffel Tower tours to street food crawls in Bangkok. You do not need to import any data or connect any external services to start building immediately.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to `/register` in your browser. Fill in the sign-up form with the following details:

    | Field | Requirement |
    |---|---|
    | Full Name | Your display name (shown on public trips) |
    | Email Address | A valid email — automatically lowercased |
    | Password | Minimum 8 characters; must include at least 1 letter and 1 number |
    | Confirm Password | Must match the password field exactly |

    Click **Create Account**. On success you are redirected to `/dashboard`.

    <Tip>
      Your email and name appear on publicly shared trip pages. Use a name you are comfortable with being visible to other travellers.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or Log In with a Demo Account">
    Want to explore the platform before committing to an account? Use one of the pre-configured demo credentials for instant access.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Demo Traveller">
        ```
        Email:    user@globetrotter.com
        Password: password123
        ```
        Full access to the Itinerary Builder, Budget Tracker, City Discovery, and Activity Catalogue. Ideal for exploring all core traveller features.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Demo Admin">
        ```
        Email:    admin@globetrotter.com
        Password: password123
        ```
        All traveller features plus access to the Admin Dashboard at `/admin` — view platform-wide metrics, trip audits, and destination analytics.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Click the **1-Click Demo Traveler Login** or **1-Click Admin Demo Login** button on the sign-in page to skip typing entirely.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Trip">
    From the Dashboard, click the **+ Plan New Trip** button in the hero banner. A creation modal opens with the following fields:

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Trip Title | 3–100 characters. Example: *Summer Euro-Trip 2026* |
    | Start Date | The first day of your journey |
    | End Date | The last day; must be on or after start date, and within **90 days** of it |
    | Target Budget | Optional numeric amount. Defaults to `0` (no budget limit enforced) |
    | Currency | Defaults to `USD`. Options include EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD |
    | Description | Optional notes or reminders for yourself or travel companions |
    | Cover Image | Optional URL or choose from the curated gallery |

    Click **Create Trip**. GlobeTrotter generates a unique URL-safe slug for your trip and redirects you to the Itinerary Builder.

    <Warning>
      Trip dates cannot exceed **90 days**. If you select a date range longer than 90 days, the form will reject it with a validation error. Adjust your end date before saving.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add City Stops">
    Inside the Itinerary Builder, the **left panel** is your Route Manager. Add stops in the order you plan to visit them.

    1. Click **+ Add City Stop** to open the City Search drawer.
    2. Search for a destination by city or country name (e.g. "Paris", "Japan").
    3. Select the destination from the results.
    4. Set the **Arrival Date** and **Departure Date** for that city. Both must fall within the trip's overall date range.
    5. Choose a **Transport Mode** for how you are arriving at this stop:

    | Mode | When to use |
    |---|---|
    | `FLIGHT` | International or domestic air travel |
    | `TRAIN` | Rail connections (Eurostar, Shinkansen, etc.) |
    | `BUS` | Intercity or overnight bus |
    | `DRIVE` | Self-drive or road trip leg |
    | `FERRY` | Sea or river crossing |
    | `NONE` | First stop, or no transit cost to record |

    6. Optionally enter an **Accommodation Cost** for the stay and a **Transport Cost** for the leg into this city.
    7. Click **Save Stop**.

    Repeat for every city on your route. GlobeTrotter auto-populates the next stop's arrival date from the previous stop's departure date to keep your timeline continuous.

    <Tip>
      Drag the handle on any stop card to reorder your route. All dates are re-validated immediately when you save a reorder.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Activities to Each Stop">
    With your stops in place, populate each city's day-by-day schedule.

    1. In the Itinerary Builder's **right panel**, select the stop you want to enrich.
    2. Click **Select Activities** on any day row to open the **Activity Catalogue** modal.
    3. Browse or filter by category: `Sightseeing`, `Food & Dining`, `Adventure`, `Culture`, or `Nightlife`.
    4. Use the cost and duration sliders to narrow results to your budget and available time.
    5. Click **Schedule to Day** on any activity card and confirm the city stop and day number.
    6. The activity appears in the day's schedule with its estimated cost, duration, and a rating badge.
    7. Optionally adjust the **start time** and override the **custom cost** directly on the activity card.

    <Info>
      You can mark activities as **Completed** while you are on the road. Completed activities are visually struck through on the day view and the public itinerary, giving travel companions live progress updates.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Your Budget">
    Navigate to the **Budget** tab inside your trip. GlobeTrotter automatically aggregates every cost you have entered — no manual totalling required.

    The Budget view shows:

    - **Budget Health Card** — your Target Budget vs. the Total Estimated Cost, with a colour-coded status badge (*Within Budget* or *Over Budget*)
    - **Category Breakdown Donut Chart** — percentage split across Lodging, Transportation, Activities, and Food & Misc
    - **Daily Spend Velocity Bar Chart** — a bar per day showing that day's expenditure against your daily target threshold; days that exceed the threshold are flagged with a warning icon
    - **Itemized Expense Table** — every line-item cost with category, date, and optional notes

    You can also manually log additional expenses (visa fees, tips, souvenirs) directly in the Itemized Expense Table using the **+ Add Expense** button. Supported categories are `LODGING`, `TRANSPORT`, `ACTIVITIES`, `FOOD`, and `MISC`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share Your Trip">
    Ready to share your itinerary? Toggle the trip to public to generate a shareable vanity URL.

    1. Open the trip's **Edit Details** panel (pencil icon on the trip card, or top-right of the Builder).
    2. Flip the **Visibility** toggle from `Private` to `Public`.
    3. GlobeTrotter generates a vanity URL in the format:
       ```
       globetrotter.app/p/your-trip-title-slug
       ```
    4. Copy the link from the **Share** tray and send it via WhatsApp, Twitter/X, or any other channel.
    5. Anyone who opens the link sees your full read-only itinerary — no login required.
    6. Logged-in viewers can click **Clone This Trip** to instantly copy your entire itinerary — all stops, activities, and budget items — into their own account for editing.

    <Tip>
      The Share tray also includes a **QR Code generator**. Download it and add it to a printed packing list, travel journal, or group chat image so companions can access the itinerary offline.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
