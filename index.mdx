Whether you're a solo backpacker watching every dollar or a family organizer juggling a complex 3-week route, GlobeTrotter gives you the tools to plan with confidence.

## How GlobeTrotter Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up with your email or use the one-click demo login to explore the platform instantly — no configuration required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan a new trip">
    Give your trip a title, set a date range and target budget, and choose a currency. GlobeTrotter auto-generates a shareable URL slug.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your itinerary">
    Search the city catalogue and add stops in order. For each stop, set arrival/departure dates, pick a transport mode, and schedule activities from the curated catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your budget">
    The budget engine automatically totals Lodging, Transport, Activities, and Food costs across all stops and shows you a variance against your target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share and collaborate">
    Toggle your trip to public and share the vanity link. Anyone can view your full itinerary and clone it to their own account with a single click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features at a Glance

| Feature | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Multi-City Builder** | Drag-and-drop stops, sequential date validation, per-stop accommodation and transport inputs |
| **Budget Engine** | Auto-aggregates 4 cost categories, shows daily burn rate, flags over-budget days |
| **City Discovery** | Full-text search + filters for continent, cost index (Budget/Moderate/Luxury), and popularity |
| **Activity Catalogue** | 80+ curated activities across 6 continents; schedule to a specific day and time slot |
| **Calendar Timeline** | Monthly/weekly grid view with draggable activity chips |
| **Public Sharing** | Vanity URL, read-only public view, one-click ACID trip clone |
| **Demo Access** | Pre-seeded demo accounts for instant hands-on evaluation |

<Note>
  GlobeTrotter ships with 20+ major global destinations and 80+ curated activities pre-loaded. You can start building a real itinerary immediately after signing up — no data import needed.
</Note>
