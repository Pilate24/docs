The Activity Catalogue gives you access to 80+ curated activities across 6 continents — filterable by category, destination, price range, and duration — and lets you schedule any activity directly to a specific day in your itinerary. Whether you're hunting for a hidden culinary experience in Tokyo or an adrenaline-fueled adventure in Queenstown, the catalogue surfaces the right options and wires them into your trip budget the moment you confirm.

## Accessing the Catalogue

You can reach the Activity Catalogue from two entry points:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Explore Menu" icon="compass">
    In the left sidebar, navigate to **Explore → Activities** to browse the full catalogue independent of any trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Itinerary Builder" icon="calendar-days">
    Inside any trip, open the **Builder** tab, locate a city stop, and click **Select Activities** to browse activities pre-filtered for that destination.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Browsing Activities

The catalogue toolbar gives you four independent filters that stack together to narrow results in real time.

**Category filter pills** — click any pill to show only activities of that type:

| Pill | Activity Type |
|---|---|
| Cultural | Museums, heritage sites, local ceremonies |
| Outdoor | Parks, scenic walks, nature reserves |
| Culinary | Food tours, cooking classes, wine tastings |
| Nightlife | Bar crawls, live music, rooftop experiences |
| Shopping | Markets, boutique districts, artisan workshops |
| Wellness | Spa, yoga retreats, meditation |
| Adventure | Hiking, water sports, extreme activities |

**Search bar** — type any keyword to match against activity titles across all categories.

**Price range slider** — drag both handles to set a minimum and maximum estimated cost in your trip's currency.

**Duration filter** — set a maximum duration in minutes to exclude activities that run longer than your available window.

## Activity Card Details

Each card in the catalogue displays at a glance:

- **Title** — the activity name and location hint
- **Category** — colour-coded badge matching the filter pills
- **Estimated Cost** — the catalogue default price (USD)
- **Duration** — estimated time in minutes
- **Star Rating** — average user rating from 1–5

## Scheduling an Activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the activity you want">
    Use the filters and search bar to locate the activity. Confirm the estimated cost and duration suit your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Schedule to Day"'>
    On the activity card, click the **Schedule to Day** button to open the scheduling modal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the trip stop (city)">
    Choose the city stop where you want this activity. The dropdown lists all stops currently in your itinerary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the date within that stop's date range">
    Pick a specific date from the calendar — only dates that fall within the chosen stop's arrival and departure dates are selectable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a start time (optional)">
    Enter a start time to place the activity as a timed block in the Calendar view. Leave blank to add it as an unscheduled item for that day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a custom cost (optional)">
    If you have a specific price — for example, a quote from a tour operator — enter it in the **Custom Cost** field to override the catalogue estimate. Your trip budget updates immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Confirm"'>
    GlobeTrotter saves the activity to the selected stop and day. You'll see it appear as a chip on the stop card in the Builder and as a timed block in the Calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Scheduled Activities

Once scheduled, activities surface in two places:

- **Itinerary Builder (Builder tab)** — activities appear as colour-coded chips on each city stop card, grouped by day.
- **Calendar Timeline (Calendar tab)** — activities appear as timed blocks on their scheduled dates, alongside the city stay spans.

## Removing an Activity

To remove a scheduled activity, use one of these methods:

- **In the Itinerary Builder** — click the **×** on the activity chip attached to a stop card.
- **Via the API** — send a `DELETE` request to the activity endpoint:

```http
DELETE /api/trips/:tripId/stops/:stopId/activities/:id
```

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "data": null,
  "message": "Activity removed from stop.",
  "error": null
}
```

<Tip>
  Use the **custom cost** override when you have a specific booking quote that differs from the catalogue estimate — for example, a private guided tour you've already reserved. Entering the actual price keeps your budget projection accurate from the moment you confirm.
</Tip>

## Activity Fields Reference

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Title | Activity name as listed in the catalogue |
| Category | Type of activity (Cultural, Outdoor, Culinary, etc.) |
| Estimated Cost | Catalogue default price in USD |
| Duration | Estimated time to complete the activity, in minutes |
| Rating | Average user rating on a 1–5 scale |
| Custom Cost | Your override price — replaces the catalogue estimate for budget calculations |
