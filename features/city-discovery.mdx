GlobeTrotter's City Discovery engine lets you search and filter a curated catalogue of global destinations, explore average daily cost information, and add cities directly to your itinerary — all without leaving the app. Whether you are building a trip from scratch or looking for inspiration, City Discovery is your starting point.

## Accessing City Discovery

You can reach the City Discovery catalogue from two entry points:

- **Sidebar** → click **Explore Cities** in the left navigation menu
- **Itinerary Builder** → type into the city search bar to trigger the catalogue inline

---

## Searching for Destinations

Type any city or country name into the search bar at the top of the catalogue. Results update in real time as you type, matching against both city names and country names. For example, typing `Japan` surfaces Tokyo alongside any other Japanese cities in the catalogue.

---

## Filtering the Catalogue

Use the two filter controls alongside the search bar to narrow results to destinations that match your trip profile:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Continent">
    Filter cities to a single continent. The available options are:

    - **Africa**
    - **Asia**
    - **Europe**
    - **North America**
    - **South America**
    - **Oceania**

    Select **All Continents** (the default) to see the full catalogue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cost Index">
    Filter by the destination's cost tier. GlobeTrotter assigns every city one of three cost index ratings:

    | Value | Label | Avg Daily Cost |
    |---|---|---|
    | `BUDGET` | Budget ($) | Under $80 / day |
    | `MODERATE` | Moderate ($$) | $80 – $200 / day |
    | `LUXURY` | Luxury ($$$) | $200+ / day |

    Select **All Budgets** (the default) to see cities across all tiers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You can combine both filters simultaneously — for example, **Asia** + **BUDGET** to find the best-value cities in Asia.

## Reading a City Card

Each result in the catalogue is displayed as a card with the following information:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="City Image & Name" icon="image">
    A curated hero photo of the destination, overlaid with the city name and country label.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Daily Cost" icon="coins">
    The estimated average spend per day for a typical traveller, displayed as `~$X/day` on the card image.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost Index Rating" icon="tag">
    The `BUDGET`, `MODERATE`, or `LUXURY` tier indicating the city's overall price level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Country" icon="flag">
    The country the city belongs to, shown as a label beneath the city name.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Adding a City to Your Itinerary

Click **Add to Itinerary** on any city card to add that destination to a trip:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add to Itinerary">
    The button is located at the bottom of every city card. Clicking it selects that city and opens the stop configuration form in the Itinerary Builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your stay dates">
    Enter the **Arrival Date** and **Departure Date** for this stop. Both dates must fall within your trip's overall date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose transport mode and costs">
    Select the **Transport Mode** to reach this city (`FLIGHT`, `TRAIN`, `BUS`, `DRIVE`, `FERRY`, or `NONE`) and enter the transport cost and nightly accommodation rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the stop">
    Click **Add to Itinerary** in the stop form to save the city to your route. The Budget Engine updates immediately to reflect the new costs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cost Index Reference

Use this table as a quick reference when planning your overall trip budget:

| Rating | Cost Index | Avg Daily Cost | Typical Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$` | **BUDGET** | Under $80 | Hostels, street food, free attractions |
| `$$` | **MODERATE** | $80 – $200 | Mid-range hotels, restaurants, paid tours |
| `$$$` | **LUXURY** | $200+ | 4–5 star hotels, fine dining, premium experiences |

## Destination Data via API

You can query the destination catalogue programmatically using the destinations endpoints:

```http
GET /api/destinations
```

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "data": [
    {
      "id": "1",
      "name": "Paris",
      "country": "France",
      "continent": "Europe",
      "costIndex": "MODERATE",
      "avgDailyCost": 150
    }
  ],
  "message": "Destinations retrieved successfully",
  "error": null
}
```

Fetch a single destination by ID:

```http
GET /api/destinations/:id
```

<Info>
  The catalogue covers major global cities across all 6 continents. New destinations are added regularly by the GlobeTrotter platform team. If you would like to request a specific city be added to the catalogue, contact support at **support@globetrotter.app**.
</Info>

## Saving Destinations to Your Bucket List

You can bookmark any city to your personal Bucket List without immediately adding it to a trip:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the city detail page">
    Click the city name to open the destination detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the bookmark icon">
    Click the **bookmark** icon in the city detail header. The city is saved to your **Saved Destinations** list in your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to a trip later">
    Visit your profile → **Saved Destinations** and click **+ Add to Trip** whenever you are ready to include that city in an itinerary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the Bucket List feature during the inspiration phase of trip planning — save all the cities you are considering, then use the Cost Index filter to shortlist destinations that fit your budget before committing to a route.
</Tip>
