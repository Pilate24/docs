GlobeTrotter's City Discovery engine lets you search and filter a curated catalogue of 20+ global destinations, explore cost information and popularity scores, and add cities directly to your itinerary — all without leaving the app. Whether you are building a trip from scratch or looking for inspiration, City Discovery is your starting point.

## Accessing City Discovery

You can reach the City Discovery catalogue from two entry points:

- **Sidebar** → click **Explore Cities** in the left navigation menu
- **Itinerary Builder** → type into the city search bar to trigger the catalogue inline

---

## Searching for Destinations

Type any city or country name into the search bar at the top of the catalogue. Results update in real time as you type, matching against both city names and country names. For example, typing `"Japan"` surfaces Tokyo alongside any other Japanese cities in the catalogue.

---

## Filtering the Catalogue

Use the three filter controls to narrow results to destinations that match your trip profile:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Continent">
    Filter cities to a single continent. The available options are:

    - **Africa**
    - **Asia**
    - **Europe**
    - **North America**
    - **South America**
    - **Oceania**

    Select **All Continents** (the default) to see the full catalogue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cost Index">
    Filter by the destination's cost tier. GlobeTrotter assigns every city one of three cost index ratings:

    | Symbol | Cost Index | Avg Daily Cost |
    |---|---|---|
    | `$` | **BUDGET** | Under $80 / day |
    | `$$` | **MODERATE** | $80 – $200 / day |
    | `$$$` | **LUXURY** | $200+ / day |

    Select **All Budgets** (the default) to see cities across all tiers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Popularity">
    Filter by popularity score to surface only the highest-rated destinations. Select **Top Rated (4.5+)** to see cities that consistently rank among travellers' favourites.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You can combine all three filters simultaneously — for example, **Asia** + **BUDGET** + **Top Rated** to find the best-value high-popularity cities in Asia.

## Reading a City Card

Each result in the catalogue is displayed as a card with the following information:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="City Image & Name" icon="image">
    A curated hero photo of the destination, overlaid with the city name and country label.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Daily Cost" icon="coins">
    The estimated average spend per day for a typical traveller, displayed as `~$X/day` on the card image.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost Index Rating" icon="tag">
    The `$` / `$$` / `$$$` symbol indicating whether the city is Budget, Moderate, or Luxury.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Popularity Score" icon="star">
    A score out of 10 reflecting how frequently this destination is added to itineraries on GlobeTrotter.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Actions on a City Card

Each city card exposes two primary actions:

### Add to Itinerary

Click **Add to Itinerary** on any city card to start adding that destination to a trip:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add to Itinerary">
    The button is located at the bottom of every city card. Clicking it selects that city and pre-fills the Itinerary Builder's city search field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your trip">
    If you have multiple trips, a prompt asks you to select which trip to add this city to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the stop details">
    The Itinerary Builder opens with the city already pre-filled. Set the arrival and departure dates, transport mode, and costs, then click **Add to Itinerary** to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Explore City

Click **Explore City** to open the full city detail page. This page lists all activities available for that destination, grouped by category, with ratings, durations, and costs — so you can plan your days before committing to a stop.

## Cost Index Reference

Use this table as a quick reference when planning your overall trip budget:

| Rating | Cost Index | Avg Daily Cost | Typical Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$` | **BUDGET** | Under $80 | Hostels, street food, free attractions |
| `$$` | **MODERATE** | $80 – $200 | Mid-range hotels, restaurants, paid tours |
| `$$$` | **LUXURY** | $200+ | 4–5 star hotels, fine dining, premium experiences |

<Info>
  The catalogue currently covers 20+ major global cities across 6 continents. New destinations are added regularly by the GlobeTrotter platform team. If you would like to request a specific city be added to the catalogue, contact support at **support@globetrotter.app**.
</Info>

## Saving Destinations to Your Bucket List

You can bookmark any city to your personal Bucket List without immediately adding it to a trip:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the city detail page">
    Click **Explore City** on the destination card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the bookmark icon">
    Click the **bookmark** icon in the city detail header. The city is saved to your **Saved Destinations** list in your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to a trip later">
    Visit your profile → **Saved Destinations** and click **+ Add to Trip** whenever you are ready to include that city in an itinerary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the Bucket List feature during the inspiration phase of trip planning — save all the cities you are considering, then use the Cost Index filter to shortlist destinations that fit your budget before committing to a route.
</Tip>
