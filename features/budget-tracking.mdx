The Budget screen gives you a live financial snapshot of your trip — total estimated cost, a breakdown by spending category, daily burn rate, and variance against the target budget you set when creating the trip. Every time you add or update a stop, activity, or cost figure in the Itinerary Builder, the budget figures recalculate instantly.

## Navigating to the Budget Screen

Open any trip from **My Trips**, then click the **Budget** tab in the trip navigation bar. The Budget Engine loads with all stops and activities already factored in.

---

## Budget Summary Cards

The top row of the Budget screen displays four key figures at a glance:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Estimated Cost" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    The sum of all lodging, transport, activity, and food costs across every stop in the trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Target Budget" icon="bullseye-arrow">
    The spending ceiling you defined when creating or editing the trip. Adjust it any time via the trip edit panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Budget Variance" icon="scale-balanced">
    The difference between your target and the estimated total. A **positive** value (shown in green) means you are under budget. A **negative** value (shown in red) means you have exceeded it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Cost / Day" icon="calendar-day">
    Total estimated cost divided by the number of trip days. Use this figure to gauge whether individual days are on pace.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Category Breakdown Donut Chart

The donut chart (powered by Recharts) visualises the proportional share of each spending category in your total budget:

| Segment | Category | What It Includes |
|---|---|---|
| 🏨 Blue | **Lodging** | Accommodation cost × nights per stop |
| ✈️ Pink | **Transport** | Transport cost entered for each stop |
| 🎟️ Teal | **Activities** | Sum of estimated costs for all scheduled activities |
| 🍽️ Orange | **Food** | Flat estimate of $50 per trip day |

Hover over any segment to see the exact currency amount and percentage share.

---

## Daily Spend Velocity Bar Chart

The bar chart shows your estimated spending broken down by individual day across the entire trip duration. Each bar represents one calendar day. Days where the projected spend exceeds the daily average threshold are highlighted with a warning indicator — these are the days to review in the Itinerary Builder to optimize costs.

<Warning>
  Days where estimated spend exceeds the daily average are flagged in the bar chart. Open the Itinerary Builder for those specific dates and consider redistributing activities or renegotiating accommodation costs to bring the day back within target.
</Warning>

## Itemized Expense Breakdown

Below the charts, the budget summary panel lists every cost component by category with its contributing stop or activity:

- Each lodging entry shows the city, nightly rate, and total nights
- Each transport entry shows the city stop and mode of travel
- Each activity entry shows the activity title and estimated ticket cost
- Food is listed as a flat daily estimate for the full trip duration

---

## How Budget Figures Are Calculated

GlobeTrotter derives all budget figures from the stops and activities you enter. The formulas below show exactly how each number is computed:

```text
Lodging Total    = Σ (accommodationCost per stop × nights at that stop)
Transport Total  = Σ transportCost per stop
Activities Total = Σ estimatedCost for all scheduled activities
Food Total       = $50 × total trip days

Total Cost       = Lodging + Transport + Activities + Food
Budget Variance  = Target Budget − Total Cost
Avg Cost / Day   = Total Cost ÷ Total Trip Days
```

<Note>
  Budget figures are estimates based on the costs you enter and the flat $50/day food default. For more granular tracking, add explicit expense records via the Expenses API (`POST /api/trips/:tripId/expenses`) with categories from `LODGING`, `TRANSPORT`, `ACTIVITIES`, `FOOD`, or `MISC`.
</Note>

## Updating Your Target Budget

Your target budget is set during trip creation, but you can revise it at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Trips">
    Navigate to **My Trips** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the edit panel">
    Click the **pencil (edit)** icon on the trip card you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the Target Budget field">
    Enter a new value in the **Target Budget** field. You can also change the currency from the dropdown (USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The Budget Variance card on the Budget screen reflects the new target immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Budget Status Indicators

| Status | Condition | Badge Colour |
|---|---|---|
| **Within Budget** | `Budget Variance ≥ 0` | Green |
| **Over Budget** | `Budget Variance < 0` | Red |

<Tip>
  Set your target budget slightly below your true ceiling — the buffer gives you room to absorb unexpected expenses like visa fees, airport transfers, or spontaneous activities without flipping to an over-budget status.
</Tip>
