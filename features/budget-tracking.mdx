The Budget screen gives you a live financial snapshot of your trip — total estimated cost, a breakdown by spending category, daily burn rate, and variance against the target budget you set when creating the trip. Every time you add or update a stop, activity, or cost figure in the Itinerary Builder, the budget figures recalculate instantly.

## Navigating to the Budget Screen

Open any trip from **My Trips**, then click the **Budget** tab in the trip navigation bar. The Budget Engine loads with all stops and activities already factored in.

---

## Budget Summary Cards

The top row of the Budget screen displays four key figures at a glance:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Estimated Cost" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    The sum of all lodging, transport, activity, and food costs across every stop in the trip.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Target Budget" icon="bullseye-arrow">
    The spending ceiling you defined when creating or editing the trip. Adjust it any time via the trip edit panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Budget Variance" icon="scale-balanced">
    The difference between your target and the estimated total. A **positive** value (shown in green) means you are under budget. A **negative** value (shown in red) means you have exceeded it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Average Cost / Day" icon="calendar-day">
    Total estimated cost divided by the number of trip days. Use this figure to gauge whether individual days are on pace.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Category Breakdown — DonutChart

The `DonutChart` component (powered by Recharts) visualises the proportional share of each spending category in your total budget:

| Segment | Category | What It Includes |
|---|---|---|
| 🏨 Blue | **Lodging** | Accommodation cost × nights per stop |
| ✈️ Pink | **Transport** | Transport cost entered for each stop |
| 🎟️ Teal | **Activities** | Sum of estimated costs for all scheduled activities |
| 🍽️ Orange | **Food** | Flat estimate of $50 per trip day |

Hover over any segment to see the exact currency amount and percentage share.

---

## Daily Spend Velocity — DailySpendBar

The `DailySpendBar` chart shows your estimated total cost averaged across the number of trip days. Each bar represents the average daily spend, giving you a quick sense of pacing relative to your target budget. Days where the projected spend exceeds the daily average threshold are highlighted with a warning indicator — these are the stops to review in the Itinerary Builder to optimise costs.

<Warning>
  When the daily spend indicator exceeds your target, open the Itinerary Builder for the flagged stops and consider redistributing activities or adjusting accommodation costs to bring the day back within target.
</Warning>

---

## Budget Variance Indicator — BudgetVariancePill

The `BudgetVariancePill` component shows the difference between your target budget and the total estimated cost in a compact badge:

| Status | Condition | Badge Colour |
|---|---|---|
| **Within Budget** | `Budget Variance ≥ 0` | Green |
| **Over Budget** | `Budget Variance < 0` | Red |

## Itemized Expense Breakdown

Below the charts, the budget summary panel lists every cost component by category:

- Each **Lodging** entry shows the nightly rate multiplied by the number of nights at that stop
- Each **Transport** entry shows the cost entered for reaching that stop
- Each **Activities** entry shows the estimated cost for each scheduled activity
- **Food** is listed as a flat `$50 × total trip days` estimate for the full trip

---

## How Budget Figures Are Calculated

GlobeTrotter derives all budget figures from the stops and activities you enter in the Itinerary Builder. The formulas below show exactly how each number is computed:

```text
Lodging Total    = Σ (accommodationCost per stop × nights at that stop)
Transport Total  = Σ transportCost per stop
Activities Total = Σ estimatedCost for all scheduled activities
Food Total       = $50 × total trip days

Total Cost       = Lodging + Transport + Activities + Food
Budget Variance  = Target Budget − Total Cost
Avg Cost / Day   = Total Cost ÷ Total Trip Days
```

<Note>
  The $50/day food estimate is a default built into the Budget Engine. For a more accurate food figure, update your accommodation costs and activity costs to reflect your planned spend — the engine aggregates everything you enter automatically.
</Note>

## Updating Your Target Budget

Your target budget is set during trip creation, but you can revise it at any time:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Trips">
    Navigate to **My Trips** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the edit panel">
    Click the **pencil (edit)** icon on the trip card you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the Target Budget field">
    Enter a new value in the **Target Budget** field. You can also change the currency from the dropdown (USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. The Budget Variance indicator on the Budget screen reflects the new target immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating Trip Budget via API

You can update the target budget programmatically using the trip update endpoint:

```http
PUT /api/trips/:id
```

```json
{
  "targetBudget": 4500,
  "currency": "EUR"
}
```

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "data": {
    "id": "trip_123",
    "targetBudget": 4500,
    "currency": "EUR"
  },
  "message": "Trip updated successfully",
  "error": null
}
```

You can also retrieve the full budget breakdown for a trip at any time:

```http
GET /api/trips/:tripId/budget
```

<Tip>
  Set your target budget slightly below your true spending ceiling — the buffer gives you room to absorb unexpected costs like visa fees, airport transfers, or spontaneous activities without flipping to an over-budget status.
</Tip>
