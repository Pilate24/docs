The Calendar & Timeline view turns your itinerary into a visual schedule so you can understand the shape of your trip at a glance. City stays appear as color-coded spans across the calendar grid, and activities appear as chips on their scheduled days. You can read your plan, check for gaps or overlaps, reschedule activities by dragging them to a new day, and drill into any date with a single click.

## Accessing the Calendar

Open a trip from **My Trips**, then click the **Calendar** tab in the trip navigation bar. The calendar loads with the current month in view, anchored to your trip's start date.

## Calendar Views

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Monthly View">
    The monthly view displays a full calendar month grid with your entire trip's shape visible at once.

    - **City stay spans** render as colored horizontal bands stretching across consecutive days — each city gets a distinct color so overlapping transitions are easy to spot.
    - **Activity chips** sit inside individual date cells, showing the activity name. If a day has more than three activities, an overflow badge (e.g., **+2 more**) appears — click it to expand.
    - **Best for:** seeing the big picture, checking how cities connect across weeks, and spotting under-scheduled or over-packed days.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Weekly View">
    The weekly view displays a 7-day grid with significantly more vertical space per day.

    - **City stay bands** still appear at the top of each column.
    - **Timed activity blocks** render as stacked cards showing the activity name, start time, and duration — similar to a calendar app's day view compressed into a week.
    - **Best for:** fine-tuning a single week, managing a dense schedule, and verifying that activity times don't conflict within a day.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reading the Calendar

Understanding the visual language of the calendar makes it faster to review your plan:

- **City span bands** stretch from arrival to departure for each stop. The band color corresponds to the city, and the city name label appears at the left edge of the span.
- **Activity chips** display the activity name. Click any chip to open a detail popover showing the full activity name, category, scheduled time, duration, and cost.
- **Overflow badges** appear when more activities are scheduled on a day than fit in the grid cell. Click the badge to reveal the full list for that day.

## Drag and Reschedule

To move an activity to a different day, drag its chip to any other date cell within the **same stop's date range**. GlobeTrotter updates the scheduled date automatically and recalculates any time-sensitive budget or conflict warnings.

<Note>
  You can only drag an activity to a date that still falls within the city stop it belongs to. To move an activity to a different city, remove it from the current stop and schedule it fresh to the new one via the Itinerary Builder.
</Note>

## Day Detail Drawer

Click any date on the calendar to open the **Day Detail Drawer** — a slide-over panel on the right side of the screen. The drawer shows the complete schedule for that day:

- All activities in chronological order, each with its start time, category icon, duration, and cost
- The city stop you're in for that date
- The daily cost subtotal for all activities combined

The Day Detail Drawer is read-optimized. To add or remove activities, close the drawer and switch to the **Builder** tab.

## Itinerary View Modes

The **structured itinerary view** — accessible from the **View Itinerary** button in the trip header — offers three display modes optimized for different reading contexts:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Timeline View" icon="timeline">
    A vertical, day-by-day reading view with connecting nodes and timestamps. Best for following your plan on the road.
  </Card>
  <Card title="City Cards View" icon="map-pin">
    Activities grouped by city stop. Best for reviewing what's planned at each destination without worrying about exact dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compact Agenda View" icon="list">
    A condensed list format showing all activities across all cities in chronological order. Best on mobile or for sharing a quick overview.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The calendar is optimized for reading and rescheduling. To add new activities or remove existing ones, use the **Itinerary Builder** (Builder tab) — any changes you make there reflect in the calendar immediately without a page reload.
</Note>
