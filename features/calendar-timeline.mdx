The Calendar & Timeline view turns your itinerary into a visual schedule so you can understand the shape of your trip at a glance. City stays appear as colour-coded spans across the calendar grid, and activities appear as chips on their scheduled days. You can read your plan, check for gaps or overlaps, reschedule activities by dragging them to a new day, and drill into any date with a single click.

## Accessing the Calendar

Open a trip from **My Trips**, then click the **Calendar** tab in the trip navigation bar. The calendar loads in the monthly view by default, anchored to your trip's start date.

## Calendar Views

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Monthly View">
    The monthly view displays a full 30-day grid with your entire trip's shape visible at once.

    - **City stay spans** render as coloured horizontal bands stretching across consecutive days — each city gets a distinct colour so overlapping transitions are easy to spot.
    - **Activity chips** sit inside individual date cells, showing the activity count for that day.
    - **Best for:** seeing the big picture, checking how cities connect across weeks, and spotting under-scheduled or over-packed days.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Weekly View">
    The weekly view displays a 7-day grid, showing one week at a time.

    - **City stay bands** appear across the top of the weekly grid to show which stop you are in on each day.
    - **Activity chips** are displayed within each day column, showing the number of activities scheduled.
    - **Best for:** fine-tuning a single week, managing a dense schedule, and verifying that activities are spread well across days.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Daily View">
    The daily view displays a single date in full detail.

    - **City stop information** shows which destination you are in for that day.
    - **All activities** for the day are listed with their titles and details.
    - **Best for:** reviewing the complete plan for one specific day before or during travel.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Reading the Calendar

Understanding the visual language of the calendar makes it faster to review your plan:

- **City span bands** stretch from arrival to departure for each stop. The band colour corresponds to the city, making it easy to see at a glance how long you spend in each destination.
- **Activity count** is displayed inside each date cell. Days with at least one activity show a count badge in blue.
- **Empty days** are shown in a neutral background — a quick visual cue that no activities are scheduled for that date yet.

## Drag and Reschedule

To move an activity to a different day, drag its chip to any other date cell within the **same stop's date range**. GlobeTrotter updates the scheduled date automatically and recalculates any time-sensitive budget or conflict warnings.

<Note>
  You can only drag an activity to a date that still falls within the city stop it belongs to. To move an activity to a different city, remove it from the current stop in the Itinerary Builder and schedule it fresh to the new stop.
</Note>

## Day Detail Drawer

Click any date on the calendar to open the **Day Detail Drawer** — a slide-over panel that shows the complete schedule for that day:

- All activities in chronological order, each with its title, category, duration, and cost
- The city stop you are in for that date
- The daily cost subtotal for all activities combined

The Day Detail Drawer is read-optimised for reviewing your plan. To add or remove activities, switch to the **Builder** tab.

## Itinerary View Modes

The **structured itinerary view** — accessible via **View Itinerary** in the trip header — offers three display modes optimised for different reading contexts:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Timeline View" icon="timeline">
    A vertical, day-by-day reading view with connecting nodes and timestamps. Best for following your plan on the road.
  </Card>
  <Card title="City Cards View" icon="map-pin">
    Activities grouped by city stop. Best for reviewing what is planned at each destination without worrying about exact dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compact Agenda View" icon="list">
    A condensed list format showing all activities across all cities in chronological order. Best on mobile or for sharing a quick overview.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  The calendar is optimised for reading and rescheduling. To add new activities or remove existing ones, use the **Itinerary Builder** (Builder tab) — any changes made there are reflected in the calendar immediately without a page reload.
</Note>
