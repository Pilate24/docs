GlobeTrotter's budget engine automatically aggregates every cost across all your stops and activities into a single, unified financial summary — no manual spreadsheets required. Every time you add a city stop, log an accommodation cost, schedule an activity, or record a transport leg, the engine recalculates your totals, category breakdowns, and budget variance in real time. This page explains exactly how those calculations work.

## Cost Categories

The budget engine tracks four distinct cost categories. Each category is sourced from a specific part of your itinerary data:

| Category | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Lodging** | `accommodationCost` per stop | Flat total cost entered per city stop |
| **Transport** | `transportCost` per stop | Per-leg cost for the transport into that city |
| **Activities** | `customCost` or `estimatedCost` per scheduled activity | `customCost` takes priority when set; otherwise `estimatedCost` is used |
| **Food** | $50/day flat rate per destination day | Auto-calculated based on nights at each stop |

<Tip>
The $50/day food estimate is a default placeholder to give your budget a realistic baseline. You can override it at any time by adding explicit `FOOD` expense records via the expense API — those records replace the flat-rate estimate in your totals.
</Tip>

## Financial Formulas

The engine derives all financial metrics from the four categories above. Here is each formula used:

### Total Estimated Cost

```text
Total Cost = Lodging + Transport + Activities + Food
```

The sum of all `accommodationCost` values across stops, all `transportCost` values across stops, all activity costs (using `customCost` where set, otherwise `estimatedCost`), and the auto-calculated food estimate ($50 × total destination days).

### Average Cost Per Day

```text
Average Cost/Day = Total Cost ÷ Total Trip Days
```

`Total Trip Days` is calculated as `(endDate − startDate) + 1`.

### Budget Variance

```text
Budget Variance = Target Budget − Total Cost
```

- A **positive** variance means you are under budget — money to spare.
- A **negative** variance means you are over budget — costs exceed your target.

### Daily Burn Rate

```text
Daily Burn Rate = [ { date, total } for each day of the trip ]
```

Each entry in the daily burn rate array represents the sum of all costs attributable to a specific calendar day. Activity costs are assigned to their `scheduledDate`; accommodation and transport costs for a stop are distributed evenly across the stop's nights.

## Budget Charts

GlobeTrotter renders three visual components to help you understand your financial picture at a glance:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Category Donut Chart" icon="chart-pie">
    A Recharts donut chart showing the percentage breakdown of Lodging, Transport, Activities, and Food across your total estimated cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Daily Spend Velocity" icon="chart-bar">
    A bar chart plotting daily spend totals across every day of your trip. Days that exceed your daily target threshold are highlighted as over-budget.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Budget Variance Pill" icon="gauge">
    A status badge showing your current variance in currency. Displayed in green when under budget and red when over budget.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Budget API Response

When you call `GET /api/trips/:tripId/budget`, the engine returns a fully computed financial summary:

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "data": {
    "targetBudget": 3500.00,
    "totalEstimatedCost": 3120.00,
    "variance": 380.00,
    "dailyAverage": 260.00,
    "breakdown": {
      "lodging": 1400.00,
      "transport": 750.00,
      "activities": 520.00,
      "foodAndMisc": 450.00
    },
    "dailyBurnRate": [
      { "date": "2026-09-12", "total": 280.00, "isOverBudget": false },
      { "date": "2026-09-13", "total": 420.00, "isOverBudget": true }
    ]
  },
  "message": "Budget calculated successfully",
  "error": null
}
```

### Response Fields

<ResponseField name="targetBudget" type="number">
  The target budget you set when creating the trip.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="totalEstimatedCost" type="number">
  The sum of all cost categories across the full trip.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="variance" type="number">
  `targetBudget − totalEstimatedCost`. Positive means under budget; negative means over budget.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="dailyAverage" type="number">
  `totalEstimatedCost ÷ totalTripDays`. Useful for comparing against a daily allowance.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="breakdown" type="object">
  An object with keys `lodging`, `transport`, `activities`, and `foodAndMisc`, each holding that category's total cost in your trip currency.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="dailyBurnRate" type="array">
  An array of `{ date, total, isOverBudget }` objects — one entry per calendar day of the trip — showing how costs are distributed day by day.
</ResponseField>

---

<Note>
All costs are displayed in the currency you selected when creating your trip (e.g., `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`). GlobeTrotter does not perform live currency conversion — all values are stored and calculated in your chosen trip currency.
</Note>
