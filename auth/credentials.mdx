GlobeTrotter supports standard email/password authentication for users who prefer to manage their own credentials. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plaintext, and all sessions are maintained via secure HTTP-only cookies that are invisible to client-side JavaScript.

## Register a New Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to /register">
    Open `/register` in your browser to access the GlobeTrotter sign-up form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Complete the following fields:

    | Field | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Full Name | Your display name across the platform |
    | Email Address | Trimmed and lowercased automatically |
    | Password | Minimum 8 characters, at least 1 letter, at least 1 number |
    | Confirm Password | Must exactly match the password field |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet the password requirements">
    Your password must satisfy all three rules before the form will submit:
    - At least **8 characters** long
    - Contains at least **1 letter** (a–z or A–Z)
    - Contains at least **1 number** (0–9)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note how your email is stored">
    GlobeTrotter automatically trims leading/trailing whitespace and converts your email address to lowercase before saving it. `User@Example.com` and `user@example.com` resolve to the same account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Account">
    Submit the form. On success, your account is created and you are redirected to `/dashboard` and logged in automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign In to an Existing Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to /login">
    Open `/login` in your browser to access the sign-in form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your registered email address and password into the respective fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign In">
    Submit the form. On success, a secure `gt_session` cookie is written and you are redirected to `/dashboard`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Validation Rules

The following validation is applied to every registration and sign-in attempt:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Email | Valid email format, trimmed of whitespace, lowercased before storage and lookup |
| Password (sign-up) | ≥ 8 characters, at least 1 letter (a–z / A–Z), at least 1 number (0–9) |
| Confirm Password | Must exactly match the password field |

## Error Messages

<Accordion title="Invalid email format">
  Returned when the email field contains a string that does not match a valid email pattern (e.g., missing `@`, missing domain). Double-check your address and try again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Password must be at least 8 characters">
  Returned when the submitted password is fewer than 8 characters long. Choose a longer password and resubmit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Incorrect email or password">
  Returned on a failed sign-in attempt. Either the email address is not registered, or the password does not match. Check both fields carefully — remember that email matching is case-insensitive, but passwords are case-sensitive.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Email already in use">
  Returned when you attempt to register with an email address that already has an account. Sign in to that account instead, or use a different email address.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Passwords are **never stored in plaintext**. GlobeTrotter uses bcrypt with a cost factor of 10 or higher for all password hashing. Even GlobeTrotter staff cannot retrieve your original password.
</Note>
