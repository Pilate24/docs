GlobeTrotter is a full-featured travel planning platform that brings every moving part of a multi-city journey — dates, city stops, activities, budgets, and collaborative sharing — into a single, cohesive workspace. Whether you are mapping out a two-week backpacking route across Southeast Asia or coordinating a family trip through Europe, this page introduces what GlobeTrotter does, who it is built for, and what you can accomplish with it.

## The Problem GlobeTrotter Solves

Planning a multi-city trip without dedicated tooling means juggling spreadsheets for budgets, notes apps for daily schedules, separate booking tabs for each city, and group chats to share the plan. Dates drift, costs pile up invisibly, and itinerary revisions ripple through everything manually.

GlobeTrotter eliminates that friction. Instead of stitching together a dozen disconnected tools, you build your entire journey in one place: add city stops in sequence, attach activities to each day, watch your budget aggregate automatically, and share a live itinerary page with anyone — no account required to view it.

## Four Core Objectives

Every feature in GlobeTrotter maps back to one of these four pillars:

| Objective | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Dream & Discover** | Browse 20+ pre-seeded global destinations and 80+ curated activities with cost ratings, descriptions, and Leaflet-powered maps. |
| **Design & Schedule** | Build a multi-city route in the Itinerary Builder, assign transport modes between stops, and schedule activities day by day. |
| **Control & Optimize** | Track your target budget against auto-aggregated costs across Lodging, Transport, Activities, Food, and Misc — with a per-day burn-rate chart. |
| **Collaborate & Share** | Toggle any trip public to get a vanity URL. Anyone can view the full itinerary and clone it into their own account with one click. |

## Who Uses GlobeTrotter

GlobeTrotter is designed around three primary traveller archetypes. You may see yourself in more than one.

| Persona | Profile | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Alex — Budget Backpacker** | Solo traveller, flexible schedule, hostel budgets, multi-country routes of 3–6 weeks | Tracks per-day spend against a strict target budget; uses the Activity Catalogue to find free and low-cost experiences |
| **Sarah — Family Planner** | Travelling with children, fixed school-holiday windows, moderate budget, 2–3 destination trips | Uses the Itinerary Builder to lock dates for each city; shares the public itinerary link with family members for review |
| **Liam — Social Travel Enthusiast** | Frequent traveller who publishes trip reports; builds detailed, activity-rich itineraries | Makes trips public, shares vanity URLs on social media, and lets followers clone his itineraries as a starting point |

## System Requirements

GlobeTrotter runs entirely in the browser — there is nothing to install. Use any modern desktop or mobile browser that meets these minimum versions.

| Browser | Minimum Version |
|---|---|
| Google Chrome | 110 |
| Mozilla Firefox | 110 |
| Apple Safari | 16 |
| Microsoft Edge | 110 |

<Info>
  **Platform limits to know before you start:**

  - A single trip can span a maximum of **90 days** from start date to end date.
  - All budget calculations use **USD** as the base reference currency. You can set any supported currency on a per-trip basis; exchange-rate display is informational.
  - The destination and activity catalogue is pre-seeded — no data import or third-party API key is required to get started.
</Info>
